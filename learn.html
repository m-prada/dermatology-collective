How can you make sense of a product label?

Product labels can be easier to understand when ingredients are grouped by their role and connected to what they do in the product and how they relate to the skin.

What to remember

Intro to Skin

Skin has three main layers with different roles. The epidermis makes new cells and melanin, the dermis contains structures such as sweat and oil glands, nerves, hair roots, and blood vessels, and subcutaneous fat cushions and insulates the body.

What to remember

All About Acne

Acne develops when pores become clogged with oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria. Hormonal changes are a common driver, some products can worsen breakouts, and picking or squeezing can increase the risk of irritation, infection, and scarring.
