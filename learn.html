Mechanism explorer

How can a normal follicle become an acne lesion?

Move through a simplified acne pathway: a normal follicle, increased sebum, clogging, and inflammation. This visual is designed to explain the biology, not to diagnose your skin.

A follicle normally allows sebum and surface cells to move outward without obstruction.
Label interpreter

How can you make sense of a product label?

Product labels can be easier to understand when ingredients are grouped by their role and connected to what they do in the product and how they relate to the skin.

Start with the label itself. First identify what is listed, then explore what those ingredients may do in the formulation.
