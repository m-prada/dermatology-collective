Connected
Learning

Dermatology information is often split between anatomy diagrams, condition pages, product labels, and ingredient databases. This platform is designed to connect those pieces into one learning system.

Learners can start with the biology, move into real-world context, and trace each claim back to its evidence instead of treating every topic as a separate resource.

About the Dermatology Collective

A student organization building dermatology education at Johns Hopkins.

The Dermatology Collective is a registered student organization at Johns Hopkins University focused on dermatology, dermopathology, education, research, and community engagement. The group brings together students interested in skin science and patient-centered communication, while creating projects that make dermatologic knowledge clearer and more accessible.

Through educational programming, collaborative projects, outreach, and interdisciplinary exploration, the Collective aims to connect foundational biology with clinical context, public education, and thoughtful scientific inquiry. This platform is one part of that mission: a space to translate dermatology into something more visual, navigable, and useful.
