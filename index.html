01

The platform

Dermatology information is often split between condition pages, anatomy diagrams, product labels, and ingredient databases. This project is designed to connect those pieces in one clear, interactive learning experience.

ExploreSee the biology, not just the definition.
ConnectMove from anatomy to conditions to real-world labels.
VerifySee where information came from and when it was reviewed.

The Collective

BUILT AT JOHNS HOPKINS

The Johns Hopkins Dermatology Collective is a student organization focused on dermatology education, research, outreach, and making high-quality skin-health information easier to understand.

Education

Build clear, interactive dermatology learning resources.

Research

Engage students with questions across dermatology and public health.

Outreach

Bring accessible skin-health education beyond the classroom.

Health education should make its sources visible.

The finished platform is designed so visitors can see where a claim came from, when a page was reviewed, and when a product record was last verified.
